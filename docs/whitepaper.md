# $WATT Protocol — Whitepaper Summary v1.0
## Vision
To create the world's first universal Energy-to-Earn ecosystem where 
every kilowatt-hour of clean energy generated by any individual anywhere 
on Earth earns a direct financial reward, and a portion of that value 
automatically funds schools, clean water, and electricity for children 
and communities who need it most.

## Problem
600 million Africans lack reliable electricity. Children study by 
candlelight. Schools cannot run computers. Girls miss school fetching 
water. Individuals who install solar panels receive no financial reward 
for their climate contribution. Carbon credits go to corporations, 
not communities.

## Solution
$WATT Protocol connects physical clean energy generation to the digital 
economy through three components:

1. WATT Smart Meter — IoT device that measures and cryptographically 
   signs energy data every 15 minutes
2. $WATT Token (ERC-20) — automatically minted on Base at a rate of 
   1 token per 1 verified KWh
3. Community Treasury — on-chain funds governed by smart contract 
   that cannot be altered or redirected

## Community Funds
15% Community Impact Fund: funds verified community infrastructure 
projects including off-grid school solar installations, water boreholes, 
and shared energy hubs.

5% Community Giveback Fund: tokens distributed directly to underserved 
communities in support of clean water, quality education, and 
electricity access. Aligned to SDGs 4, 6, and 7.
